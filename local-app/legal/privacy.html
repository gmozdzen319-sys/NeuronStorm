1. Who is responsible

The operator identified above is responsible for personal data processed to run Neuron Storm. Use the contact above for privacy questions and rights requests. This notice describes the application; wallet providers and other external services have their own privacy notices.

2. Information we process and why

  • Accounts: wallet address, creation time, nickname, optional names and chosen topics, to create your profile, authenticate you and route questions.
  • Conversations: questions, answers, revisions, Debate messages, votes, blocks, saved answers and reports, to provide discussions, moderation and reputation features.
  • Security and agreements: one-time challenges, verified message signatures, sessions, accepted document versions and timestamps, to establish account control, protect the service and demonstrate the agreement.
  • Payments: public transaction hashes, addresses, amounts and confirmation records, to validate paid actions, tips and rewards and prevent reuse of transaction evidence.
  • Activity: presence, read status, notification state and a browser identifier for approximate weekly visitor counts.
  • Network information: IP addresses are available to the server and hosting infrastructure for delivering requests, security and, if you opt in, approximate country estimation for the globe. The application's country lookup uses a local database, rather than sending your IP to a geolocation service.

Where data-protection law requires a lawful basis, processing necessary to provide your requested account and service is based on the contract; security, abuse prevention, dispute records and proportionate service administration rely on legitimate interests, subject to your rights. Legal obligations may require particular records. Optional globe sharing relies on your separate choice and can be turned off at any time. Agreement to the Terms of Use is not consent to every possible use of personal data.

3. Who can see information

Authorized conversation participants see questions, answers and Debate. Nicknames, topics, stars and ranking activity can appear in community views. The administrator can access conversations, reports, the formal archive and member wallet addresses for administration and manual rewards. Only the question author can save an answer. Saved Answers remain available to that author after a conversation closes.

The globe shows aggregate approximate countries, not precise coordinates or named individual locations. Presence and visitor counts are approximate. Blockchain addresses and token transfers are public on Quai, and payment recipients can see the sending address.

Infrastructure providers, including Render hosting, process information needed to operate the service. Wallet providers process requests you approve; Quai RPC services receive blockchain queries; Quainance supplies market information. External links take you to services with their own policies. Providers may process data outside your country. Any legally required safeguards for international transfers must be established by the operator before the service is offered under this notice.

4. Cookies and device storage

The app uses an eight-hour session cookie and a five-minute sign-in challenge cookie for authentication. A seven-day visitor cookie supports approximate weekly unique-browser counts. Your browser may store account-specific question drafts, sound and Debate preferences, and pending payment details. Drafts are restored for up to seven days; browser-stored values can otherwise remain until removed or browser data is cleared. Optional country sharing is stored with your account. The operator must apply any consent requirements applicable to non-essential device storage; signing these terms does not replace such consent.

5. Retention and closure

Seven-day question expiry or accepting an answer hides the conversation from normal member lists; it does not erase the underlying history. Formal question and answer revisions remain in the private administrative archive. Saved Answers, payment evidence, moderation records and agreement evidence may also remain. Debate is not in the formal text export but is stored as conversation data.

Expired sessions and sign-in challenges are cleared during operation. Live presence uses short time windows; weekly visitor records are retained for approximately eight weeks. Other account and content records currently have no automatic fixed deletion deadline. The operator must review continued retention against the purpose, disputes, security needs and applicable legal obligations, and handle deletion requests accordingly. Backup or host logs may follow separate infrastructure retention schedules. Public blockchain records cannot be erased by the platform.

6. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, turn off optional globe sharing, change sound preferences, clear local drafts, block members and report content. Contact the operator to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object to processing where those rights apply. The operator may ask for proportionate proof that you control the account, but will never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. Privacy requests do not require paying the 1 NS answer-editing fee.

Erasure may be limited by legal duties or justified retention of dispute and security records; the operator must explain any applicable limitation. Withdrawing optional consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. You may complain to the data-protection authority available in your jurisdiction. The service is intended for adults aged 18 or over; contact the operator if you believe a child has supplied personal data.

7. Changes and contact

Updated notices will identify their version and effective date. Material changes will be brought to members' attention. Contact the operator above for clarification about processing, recipients, international transfers or retention before sharing sensitive information.
